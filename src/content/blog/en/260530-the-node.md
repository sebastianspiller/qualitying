## Doing the Paperwork

Ok, I just jam around here with some letters. But while doing this I remembered the facinating idea of content and context - and the interconnection between both. I always enjooyed connections, between people for instance. Or meaning, which is the connection of words.

## Meaningwhile

Meanwhile being a developer, I can be creative and create new meanings, new connections. I might be very associative, so that connnections become to dense or loose, I mean: I could interconnect one Zen poem in a book with learning for school, which is quite loose as the jump is quite far, but it is also quite dense, as a zen poem can mean *everything*, you get the point aoubt deciding between dense and loose?


## Cacophony

Meaningwhile the world evolves so fast, I make some noise as another voice. And this worry makes me think about structure. So this is still quite dense information. Structure, we all talk about structure and want a safe place. We build countries and work with each other with *rules* so we can go to the city by car. That's structure, but how to establish it in a text? That's wehere the **node** concept comes in: You can hook into every idea, every thought, as long as it makes sense, that is it is either joyful or helpful (where as joyful and helpful are playing together, too).  And by entering a realm of thoughts you can join a play like a song and guide the discussion to where you want. It is helpful as long as the receiver gets your point.

## Meta meat

Does this jam about information make sense? Can it improve my understanding and my behaviour while talking? Or does it make me more quiet? Again, I need not to be worried. I wonder what happens to my brain cells, can they build up to a nice structured meat ball, that helps me in the world or does it make crazy? I mean, it's joyful, so it is some sort of therapy. But I cannot say if I can remember here something. It's still a brain storm – still between the pure creativity process and the need to create something meaningful, what's for the living. But how do I get away from this needy thing – or do I have to loose greed as a feeling anyway?

## Greed

The feeling which helps the animal to have food for the living. Might we loose this as no longer needed or use it as a rush of blood to the head? Is it a tasty feeling? I mean, I want to publish here my thoughts to be read by you, too. It's an extra thrill to me.
