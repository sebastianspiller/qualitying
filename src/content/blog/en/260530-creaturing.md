## Prelude

This is like the upbeat, the start, the first notes in the song. You start typing on the white canvas and look, what happens. Just not being afraid about the white blank page. Ah an associations run through the brain – a song from Mumfold & Sons called White blank Page. Winter, snow, different sort of fealings, some way of cheerish sadness. And so quickly, you run into the flow again.

The Prelude is a mighty thing, it brings you in. You might not know where it goes and where you head to and what you avoid. But have you ever tried to plan something? Isn't planning such sames thing of *figuring out*? And you grow by surprising yourself, again and agin.

## The Play with Allan Turing

You know Allan? Or the Turing test? The idea, that you don't know if you are interacting with a human or a machine? Anyways, I created the word **creaTuring**, the idea behind: We play games with AI, the machines. Not just telling "do my homework", but creating cool things by learning from each other. Finally the border between who created the results dissolve, it remains the process of creation.
