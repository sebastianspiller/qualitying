Welcome to my little corner of the internet. This is the first post on a blog
where I write about whatever I happen to be thinking about — sometimes in
English, sometimes in German.

## Why bilingual?

I think in two languages, and forcing everything into one of them always feels
like a compromise. So here, each language has its own section: English posts
live under `/en/`, German posts under `/de/`. Use the **EN / DE** switch in the
top-right corner to jump between them.

## What to expect

- Short notes and longer essays
- Experiments and things I'm learning
- The occasional rabbit hole

Thanks for reading. More soon.
